Research Framework

Consensus-Reaching Process Support with LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems

My research focuses on establishing a principled framework for LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) to support credible and interpretable consensus-reaching.

01

Structured Facilitation

Demonstrating practical online deliberation by embedding LLM-based agents into explicit multi-phase workflows.

02

Explainable Credibility Judgment

Making the results of LLM-based MAS decision-making explainable by designing an aggregation mechanism rather than solely depending on "LLM-as-a-judge".

03

Fuzzy Preference Elicitation & Analysis

Bridging linguistic preferences and formal analysis through fuzzy preference elicitation and tolerance-aware judgment.

04

Cross-Paradigm Evaluation

Formalising the multi-issue negotiation framework for LLM-based MAS to evaluate stability, cost, and fairness across mechanism-governed and prompt-based agents.
